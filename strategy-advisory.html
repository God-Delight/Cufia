STRATEGY ADVISORY

Make the big choices with confidence, and turn them into results.

Cufia helps leaders set a clear direction, choose where to focus and build the plan to get there. Then we stay to help you deliver it.

Let’s talk

THE CHALLENGE

The world is changing faster than most strategies.

Markets are shifting, technology is moving quickly and competition can come from anywhere. Many organisations have a strategy document, but it doesn’t guide daily decisions. Priorities compete, resources are spread too thin, and the gap between plan and results keeps growing. For public institutions, the pressure to deliver more with limited budgets makes clear choices even more critical.

How we help

01

Corporate & Business Strategy

Set a clear direction.

We help you define where to play and how to win, with a strategy grounded in market insight, your capabilities and your ambitions, so every part of the organisation pulls the same way.

02

Growth & Market Entry

Find your next source of growth.

We identify and assess new markets, segments, products and partnerships, and build practical entry plans that balance opportunity with risk, across Africa and beyond.

03

Customer Strategy

Put customers at the heart of your growth.

We use research and data to understand what your customers value, then shape propositions, segments and channel strategies that build loyalty and lift revenue.

04

Innovation Strategy

Build tomorrow’s advantage today.

We help you decide where to innovate, set up the structures and funding to test new ideas, and turn the most promising ones into new sources of value.

05

Financial Performance & Value Creation

Strengthen your bottom line.

We identify the levers that drive revenue, margin and cost efficiency, and build a clear plan to improve financial performance and long-term value.

06

Strategy Execution & Transformation Office

Turn strategy into results.

We translate your strategy into priorities, targets and initiatives with clear owners, and set up a transformation office that tracks progress and keeps delivery on course.

What you can expect

  • A clear, focused strategy your leadership team and board can rally behind
  • Sharper choices about where to invest, and where not to
  • Growth opportunities assessed with evidence, not assumptions
  • Measurable targets linked to customer, financial and innovation goals
  • A practical roadmap with owners, milestones and dashboards to track progress
  • Support to deliver the strategy, not just design it

Why Cufia

Our team brings strategy and management consulting experience from the Big Four, global financial institutions and African consultancies. That experience is combined with expertise in AI, data, digital and operations, so your strategy reflects how technology is reshaping your industry. We don’t just hand over a strategy document. We help you put it into action and deliver results.

How is your approach different from traditional strategy consulting?

We don’t stop at the recommendations. We help you turn strategy into priorities, initiatives and measurable targets, and our team with capabilities across strategy, AI, products, operations and project management support delivery.

How long does a strategy engagement take?

It depends on scope. A focused strategy review typically takes four to eight weeks, while a full corporate strategy and roadmap may take two to four months. We’ll agree the timeline with you upfront.

Do you work with public sector institutions?

Yes. We help ministries, agencies and state-owned enterprises set strategic priorities, design reform programmes and track delivery against public goals.
